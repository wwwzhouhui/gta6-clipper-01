A 17-second vertical GTA 6 clip cut for make.fun's "Clip GTA 6 for the most YouTube views" bounty: the best 8 seconds of Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 2 — Jason and Lucia on a jet ski, a bike vs a low-flying plane, a night chase through Vice City — cropped to 9:16, with a "GTA 6 IS HERE" hook and a make.fun pitch on the end card (spoken and on screen). Places go by the video's YouTube views.

The clip: `clip/gta6-clip-final.mp4` is not shipped here — the video lives on YouTube and `VIDEO.json` names its address. The entry program `main.ts` copies `VIDEO.json` to `/output` for the gate.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
